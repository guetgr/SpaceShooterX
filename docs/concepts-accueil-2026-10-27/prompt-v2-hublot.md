Use case: ui-mockup. Substantially redesign the supplied portrait AGP space game home concept according to this correction: a LINEAR sequence of missions with convincing THREE-DIMENSIONAL depth around the planet, viewed through a REAL large hangar observation window. Keep the industrial pixel-art universe, not the flat orbital mission carousel of the reference. Produce one polished 9:16 portrait mobile screen, no phone mockup.
Art direction: richly crafted crisp pixel-art with volumetric lighting, detailed gray utility metal, restrained safety yellow, amber maintenance lighting, cold blue Earth light. Camera is inside a spacious orbital service hangar looking through a massive panoramic rounded octagonal porthole, deeply recessed thick window surround, visible bevels, inner gasket, reflected lamp on the glass, a few foreground pipes at side and a sloping physical control console across the bottom. Clear depth layers: dark hangar foreground, glass plane with very faint reflections, exterior orbital objects at different distances, huge three-dimensional Earth with cloud shadows and terminator, distant stars. Window frame curved/rounded octagonal, NOT rectangular monitor, NOT screen bezel. No grid across Earth.
Planet and mission route dominate about 60% of the screen. Earth slightly above center, its night-side visible, convincing sphere. Missions form ONE OPEN ordered route 01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06, without branches, no closed circular ring, no row of circular cards. Preserve unambiguous sequential numbering. Six small physical orbital landmarks (satellite, debris field, scrap platform, maintenance buoy, industrial wreck, distant incident station), no enclosing circular portraits. Near landmarks visibly bigger and higher contrast, far landmarks smaller and dimmer; trajectory has perspective foreshortening. Mission 01 selected with yellow corner brackets and a small floating 01 tag; 02-06 muted with small lock symbols and readable floating number tags. Make the sequence a believable winding journey with a beginning and an end, not evenly spaced on a flat ellipse. The route is a navigation overlay visible on window glass, the objects feel situated in outer space.
Very slim top bar: AGP yellow badge, EMPLOYE 0042, components 0, settings. Small left aligned sector heading 'SECTEUR 01' 'ORBITE TERRESTRE' '0 / 6 ORDRES'. No large top ticker. Foreground sloped console lower 25% holds a CLEAN compact flat dark UI panel integrated into the hardware, exact text 'ORDRE 01 — FORMATION', 'Satellites, débris et incident majeur.', short row 'RISQUE FAIBLE' 'PRIME +5', small NORMA line 'Merci de ne rien casser.' and one prominent yellow 'PRENDRE LE SERVICE' button. Bottom narrow tabs 'ATELIER' 'MISSIONS' 'R&D' with Missions selected. Readable condensed geometric UI typography, offwhite text, much less UI density and metal trim than reference. No new gameplay mechanics, no shop, no unrelated people. Emphasize expansive window view and breathtaking orbital perspective, physically inside hangar not merely a decorative frame.

- Proposition d'interaction : sélectionner un ordre fait tourner la vue pour le ramener au premier plan ; navigation précédent/suivant pour les étapes hors champ.
