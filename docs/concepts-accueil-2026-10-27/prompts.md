## Planche de sprites

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE curated game sprite exploration sheet for the AGP space janitor mobile game, matching the industrial pixel-art garage in the reference. This is an art direction sprite proposal board, not a finished game screen and not a packed animation atlas. Landscape 3:2 composition on solid very dark graphite #070A09. Crisp deliberate pixel clusters, readable silhouettes, dark steel, chipped gray paint, tiny safety-yellow panels, local cyan indicator lamps, copper detail. Six evenly spaced cells with generous breathing room, two rows of three. Clean large white headings in French above each object:
1 'TERMINAL ORBITAL': a LIGHTWEIGHT pair of narrow worn metal side rails and a small bottom ledge, open empty center with NO screen content, no Earth, subtle brackets, suitable to frame a mission map without overpowering it.
2 'SATELLITE DE RECUPERATION': charming industrial satellite/drone, helmet-like yellow maintenance cap, one optical lens, two damaged blue solar panels, clearly a machine not a humanoid, front three-quarter view.
3 'BALISES DE MISSION': four tiny simple consistent square corner-bracket marker variations presented side by side: subdued gray available, yellow selected, muted green check complete, dark steel lock. Keep symbols sharply readable; no numerals.
4 'LAMPE DE MAINTENANCE': the same wall-mounted amber service lamp shown off and on, two states, chunky steel housing and protected warm light strip, isolated no wall.
5 'NAVETTE AGP': small white-gray utilitarian delivery van spaceship, yellow AGP panel, cyan thrusters, top-down miniature navigation sprite, recognizable cabin and cargo body, not a fighter jet.
6 'COMPOSANTS': compact salvage container with bolts and copper coil, a few distinct mechanical scraps, clearly industrial resources not gold currency coins.
Objects separated, no overlapping shadows, no connecting UI panels. Board title small 'AGP / KIT ACCUEIL' and subtitle 'EXPLORATION DE SPRITES'. No other text. Elegant restrained material richness, no huge neon bloom, no glossy 3D. These are sprites to consider producing; emphasize variety of functional assets and consistent pixel density.
