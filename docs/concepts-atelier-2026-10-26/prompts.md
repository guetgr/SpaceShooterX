## Planche des évolutions

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished landscape pixel art game art direction board showing THREE evolutionary stages of the SAME space utility van, side by side, equal size and identical front three-quarter camera. Reference 1 is existing AGP game UI for dark industrial yellow identity; reference 2 is the actual top-down player van, preserve its elongated grey utility body, dark windshield, battered silver panels and cyan lights, reinterpret in three-quarter. This is a flying SPACE VAN, no road tires. Authentic crisp visible pixel clusters and stepped edges, restrained palette, hand-crafted premium pixel art, no smooth 3D render or painted blur. Dark charcoal plain backdrop, minimal maintenance platform under each vehicle. Stage 1 original humble grey service van with yellow AGP marking, tiny thrusters. Stage 2 visibly same van with huge U-shaped industrial electromagnet on a bolted mechanical arm, copper coils and cables, side guards; three small gold coins being pulled toward magnet. Stage 3 same recognizable cabin now war machine with heavy bolted armor, larger thrusters, magnet and one industrial roof cannon; readable silhouette, no excessive tiny weapon clutter. Offwhite steel, charcoal, industrial mustard yellow, selective cyan lamps and copper coils. Clear space between vehicles, generous margins. Title exactly "AGP — DU SERVICE AU COMBAT". Below vehicles labels exactly "01 UTILITAIRE", "02 MODIFIE", "03 MACHINE DE GUERRE". Tiny top-down silhouette below each to demonstrate gameplay translation. No other text. This is concept art for a mobile game, not an advertisement.
